Book Clipper

Select pages from an Internet Archive book and save them as a clean multi-page PDF.

Choose the volume containing the pages you were reading.
Maximum 4 pages. They appear in exactly the order entered. Example: 500, 501, 1.
The saved PDF will contain one selected book page per PDF page. If you want two book pages side-by-side afterward, open the saved PDF, print it, and choose 2 pages per sheet.
