Words That Endure: Prophetic Hadith and Early Islamic Wisdom

Words That Endure is a carefully curated collection of 43 Prophetic Hadiths, 11 sayings of the Companions, and 2 narrations from the early Muslims, selected to present enduring principles that shape a Muslim's worship, character, and daily life. Rather than attempting to be comprehensive, this collection is intentionally brief, bringing together words that can be remembered easily, returned to often, and carried throughout life as enduring reminders that shape the way a Muslim worships, reflects, perseveres, and lives.

Drawn primarily from the most authentic Hadith collections and complemented by carefully selected reports from the early generations, these narrations invite reflection, strengthen faith, and encourage a life rooted in the remembrance of Allah, sincerity, patience, gratitude, and righteous action.

50 Hadith of an-Nawawi and Ibn Rajab

This collection of fifty Prophetic Hadiths was originally compiled by an-Nawawi and later completed by al-Hafidh Ibn Rajab. It gathers foundational teachings on faith, worship, character, and daily life through the words of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.

Presented in Arabic and English side by side, this edition has been carefully reviewed and produced from the original source material. The wording of each narration has been verified, references expanded, and, where appropriate, a small number of narrations have been replaced with stronger or more widely accepted reports conveying the same foundational principle in light of scholarly discussion concerning their attribution. The editorial methodology is explained in the preface.

Whether used for personal study, family reading, memorization, or teaching - this collection is a gateway to knowledge and a companion for anyone seeking to live by the guidance of the Prophet ﷺ.
