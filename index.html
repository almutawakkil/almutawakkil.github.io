About this library

These translations are produced through Taybah Press — a small effort to bring classical Islamic texts into accessible English for readers who may not have access to published editions or who live in places where Islamic books are difficult to obtain. Everything here is free. May Allāh accept it.

Al-Mutawakil

Translator, publisher, and student of Islamic knowledge. The YouTube channel al-Mutawakkil features daw'ah content, Islamic education, and reflections — with a strong following particularly in North Africa.
