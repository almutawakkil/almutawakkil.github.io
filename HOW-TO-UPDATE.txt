# HOW TO USE YOUR AL-MUTAWAKIL WEBSITE
# =======================================

## YOUR FILES
- index.html         ← the main page (books + articles)
- article-template.html  ← copy this whenever you write a new article
- /pdfs/             ← put your PDF files here

---

## HOW TO ADD YOUR 12 BOOKS
Open index.html in any text editor (Notepad on Windows, TextEdit on Mac).

For each book, find the section that says:
  <div class="book-title">Title of Book One</div>

Replace:
  - "Title of Book One"  →  actual book title
  - "ʿAqīdah"            →  the category (ʿAqīdah / Hadith / Fiqh / History)
  - "pdfs/book1.pdf"     →  the filename of your PDF (put it in the /pdfs/ folder)
  - the Amazon URL       →  your actual Amazon listing URL

---

## HOW TO ADD COVER IMAGES
1. Save your book cover as a .jpg file (e.g. cover-book1.jpg)
2. Put it in the same folder as index.html
3. In index.html, find the book's <div class="book-cover"> block
4. Replace:
     <div class="book-cover-placeholder">...</div>
   With:
     <img src="cover-book1.jpg" alt="Book title here">

---

## HOW TO WRITE AN ARTICLE
1. Make a copy of article-template.html
2. Rename it (e.g. article-tawakkul.html)
3. Open it and:
   - Change the <title> tag to your article title
   - Change the <h1> to your article title
   - Change the date
   - Replace the placeholder text with your writing
4. In index.html, add a link in the Articles section:
     <a href="article-tawakkul.html" class="article-item">
       <span class="article-title">Your article title</span>
       <span class="article-meta">Jun 2026</span>
     </a>

## HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (FREE — takes about 5 minutes)

### Step 1: Create a GitHub account
Go to https://github.com and sign up (free).

### Step 2: Create a new repository
- Click the green "New" button
- Name it exactly:  al-mutawakil  (or any name you like)
- Make it Public
- Click "Create repository"

### Step 3: Upload your files
- Click "uploading an existing file" (on the new repo page)
- Drag in: index.html, article-template.html, and your /pdfs/ folder
- Click "Commit changes"

### Step 4: Enable GitHub Pages
- Go to Settings → Pages
- Under "Source", choose: Deploy from branch
- Branch: main, Folder: / (root)
- Click Save

### Step 5: Your site is live!
After 1-2 minutes, your site will be at:
  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/al-mutawakil/

Share this link with anyone in the world — it's completely free, forever.

## WANT A CUSTOM DOMAIN? (optional, ~$12/year)
If you want the site to be at something like:
  www.al-mutawakil.com   or   www.taybahpress.com

1. Buy a domain from Namecheap.com (~$12/year)
2. In GitHub Pages settings, enter your custom domain
3. In Namecheap, add a CNAME record pointing to your GitHub Pages URL
